Drum samples in this folder are derived from "Virtuosity Drums"
by Versilian Studios and Karoryfer Samples
(recorded at Virtuosity Musical Instruments, Boston; drummer Austin McMahon).
Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Changes: several microphone positions were mixed together, trimmed, faded,
loudness-matched and encoded to AAC (.m4a) for use on the web.
